Key Finding — Large Directed Cycle A 270-node strongly-connected component spans roughly 10×16 miles of the the city network. This is not a physical loop — gravity can't circulate. It's a logical cycle created by misdirected pipes stitching the real tree into a circuit. Within it: 17 pipes with invert conflicts, 9 with negative slope, 42 with no invert data. Null FROMMH or TOMH

Trigger
Pipe has a null value in the FROMMH or TOMH field, so flow direction is unknown.

If the pipe also has usable invert elevations (UPSTREAMIN / DOWNSTREAM), it's marked QA_STATUS = "direction_inferrable" in the repaired shapefile. The graph builder (Phase 3) will recover direction from those elevations — higher invert end = upstream.

If no invert data is available, it's marked QA_STATUS = "flagged" and needs manual attention. the city had 76 pipes with null FROMMH and 59 with null TOMH (112 distinct flagged pipes after overlap).

Critical
network_qa_flags.csv network_qa_maps.pdf

Water would flow uphill

Trigger
UPSTREAMIN elevation < DOWNSTREAM elevation — the upstream end is lower than the downstream end, which means the recorded direction is backward.

The most actionable direction flag. These pipes are the primary suspects inside the 270-node directed cycle — removing the 17 worst invert conflicts collapsed that SCC by nearly half.

Phase 2 flags only. The pipe direction in the shapefile is not changed here — correction happens in Phase 3 or via manual GIS edit.

Trigger
The SLOPE field is < 0.

Cross-checked against invert elevations. Rare (2 in the city). Often accompanies or confirms an invert conflict but can also be a data entry error in the slope field alone.

Low volume — review individually; these are likely quick fixes.

Connectivity Flags
snap_gap
Warning
network_qa_flags.csv network_qa_maps.pdf

Near-miss endpoint that won't auto-merge

Trigger
Two pipe endpoints are farther apart than the snap tolerance (auto-merge threshold) but closer than 2× that tolerance — in the "should be connected but isn't" band.

Sub-tolerance gaps are silently merged by the graph builder (no flag). Gaps in the band (snap_tol, 2×snap_tol] are flagged because they're likely real disconnections, not digitizing noise. 13 found in the city — look for pipes that appear visually connected in GIS but have a small gap.

Detected using a scipy cKDTree over endpoints (O(n log n)) rather than a spatial self-join, which was too slow on ~76k endpoints.

Small circular flow path (local flip)

Trigger
A strongly-connected component (SCC) of ≤ max_mappable_cycle_nodes (default: 10) nodes is found in the directed graph.

A small cycle almost always means one or two locally misdirected pipes. Generates a zoomed PDF map page showing the cycle with flow direction arrows — quick to fix in GIS. the city had three small SCCs (4, 3, and 2 nodes).

See also large_cycle for the pathological case.

Large circular flow path (systematic error)

Trigger
A strongly-connected component exceeds max_mappable_cycle_nodes — too large to map usefully on a single page.

A large SCC is not a physical loop; it's a logical one created by systematically misdirected pipes. No PDF map is generated (a citywide tangle map is unreadable). Instead, large_cycle_suspects.csv lists all member pipes with invert/slope suspects sorted to the top — the highest-value targets for manual review.

the city's 270-node SCC is the primary instance. Will corrupt any sewershed traced through it until resolved.

Attention
network_qa_flags.csv

Subgraph not connected to the main network

Trigger
A group of pipes forms a valid subgraph but has no path connecting it to the largest connected component.

Could indicate a genuinely separate system (a private collection system, a pressure main misclassified as gravity), a snap gap that cuts off a branch, or a data error. The flag CSV includes the component size. 345 flagged in the city — most are probably small isolated branches.

CSV-only. Not PDF-mapped because the volume (~345) is too high for useful review that way.

isolated_manhole
Info
network_qa_flags.csv

Manhole with no connected pipes

Trigger
A manhole FACILITYID appears in the manholes layer but is not referenced by any pipe's FROMMH or TOMH field.

Most likely causes: access manholes or cleanouts with no associated pipe segment, manholes in areas not yet digitized, or ID mismatches between the manholes and gravity mains layers. 522 in the city — the largest single flag category but lowest operational risk (these are assets, not broken topology).

CSV-only. Doesn't affect traversal unless a target manhole is one of these — which would produce a zero-result sewershed (caught by the Phase 6 no_upstream_found flag).

Parcel straddles the pipe buffer edge

Trigger
A parcel is partially inside and partially outside the upstream pipe buffer — its inclusion in the sewershed is ambiguous.

These parcels are included (area-weighted or by centroid, per config) but flagged for human review. Common along the sewershed perimeter where a property connects to both an upstream and a downstream pipe segment.

Traversal returned zero upstream edges

Trigger
The upstream graph traversal from the target manhole found no upstream edges.

Likely causes: the target manhole is isolated (no connected pipes), the manhole ID wasn't matched, or all connecting pipes are downstream-only from this point. Output sewershed will be empty. Check whether the target is an isolated_manhole.

Few parcels matched despite upstream pipe coverage

Trigger
Upstream pipes cover a non-trivial area but the spatial join returns very few parcels.

Can mean the pipes run through undeveloped or industrial land (fine), or that the pipe buffer distance is too small to capture served parcels (check config). Review debug layers to distinguish.

QA_STATUS Field Values (gravity_mains_repaired.shp)
QA_STATUS Meaning Graph builder action
original Pipe passed all QA checks. FROMMH/TOMH present and consistent. Add edge using FROMMH → TOMH as-is.
flagged Pipe has a detected issue (invert conflict, cycle membership, snap gap, etc.). Direction may be wrong. Add edge using recorded FROMMH → TOMH, but traverse results through flagged pipes should be treated with caution.
direction_inferrable FROMMH or TOMH is null, but invert elevations (UPSTREAMIN / DOWNSTREAM) are present and usable. Assign flow direction from geometry + inverts (higher invert = upstream end), then add edge. Does not write back to shapefile unless explicitly configured.
